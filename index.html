Our Café Story

From Chikmagalur Hills to UPES Bidholi

The Velvet Bean Roastery & Café started with a simple idea: Dehradun students and coffee lovers deserve freshly roasted single-estate coffee and warm bakery treats without big-city markups.

Every morning at 6:00 AM, our bakers pull flaky croissants and rich chocolate pastries from the oven while our roaster prepares small 12 kg batches of Arabica beans.

Our Café Story

Founded in 2026 in Dehradun, The Velvet Bean Roastery & Café brings direct-trade Chikmagalur estates together with artisanal pastry and sourdough baking. Every takeaway cup, lid, and pastry box is 100% plastic-free and compostable.
